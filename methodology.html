Automated Hydro-Remote Sensing Pipeline

Profile-Driven Engine

The Mekong Sense Data Portal operates a 4-step physics-anchored pipeline that reconstructs continuous, geodetically consistent hydraulic time series (H, A, V, Qin, Qout) for Mekong basin reservoirs. The computational engine adheres strictly to physical conservation laws and profiles without hardcoded assumptions.

01

Multi-Sensor Earth Observation

  • ▪
    Optical Imagery (Sentinel-2, Landsat): Modified Normalized Difference Water Index (MNDWI) combined with Otsu adaptive thresholding to extract observed water surface area Aobs.
  • ▪
    Synthetic Aperture Radar (Sentinel-1): Height Above Nearest Drainage (HAND) mountain shadow suppression and Dempster-Shafer evidential fusion for cloud-penetrating water delineation.
  • ▪
    Satellite Altimetry (SWOT, ICESat-2): Geoid-referenced water surface elevation Halt validated with 3-tier filtering (Point-in-Polygon, Crest bounds, and Area-Elevation bounds).
Input: S1, S2, Landsat, SWOT Yield: Aobs(t), Halt(t)
02

Geodetic Bathymetry & Invariants

  • ▪
    Differential Geodetic Identity: Enforces physical conservation identity dV/dE ≡ Aphys(E) everywhere along the bathymetric curve.
  • ▪
    Shape-Preserving Spline: Monotonic Piecewise Cubic Hermite Interpolating Polynomial (PCHIP) eliminates unphysical overshoot and ensures positive curvature.
  • ▪
    Strict Operational Bounds: Enforces physical bounding: Dead Water Level (DWL) ≤ H(t) ≤ Normal Pool Level (NPL) ≤ Dam Crest.
Invariants: dV/dE ≡ A(E) Zero Inversion Mismatch
03

Catchment Hydrology & Routing

  • ▪
    Rainfall-Runoff Forcing: Basin-wide precipitation forcing from GPM IMERG and ERA5-Land reanalysis calibrated with WMO ground station benchmarks.
  • ▪
    Hydrodynamic Cascade Delay: Incorporates celerity-based routing travel time τ = L / v between cascaded reservoirs on the Lancang mainstem.
  • ▪
    Inflow Prior Estimation: Yields continuous daily upstream discharge Qin(t) accounting for upstream releases and lateral incremental inflows.
Drivers: ERA5-Land, GPM, Routing Yield: Qin(t)
04

Mass Conservation & Outflow

dS/dt = Qin(t) - Qout(t) + P(t) - ET(t) - Qloss
  • ▪
    Strict Residual Outflow Solver: Evaluates daily reservoir volume change rate dS/dt = dV/dt to solve for actual discharge Q*out(t).
  • ▪
    Physical Hydraulic Clamping: Restricts outflow to realistic turbine and spillway capacities: 0 ≤ Qout(t) ≤ Qcapacity, precluding negative fluxes.
  • ▪
    Zero Residual Verification: Verifies continuous daily mass balance with near-zero unaccounted residual across the full multi-year record.
Constraint: 0 ≤ Qout ≤ Qcap Yield: Q*out(t), ΔS(t)
